### 🌊 AquaLens — Water Quality Intelligence Platform
**Buriganga River, Dhaka, Bangladesh**  
🔗 [Live Demo](https://salek.shinyapps.io/aqualens/)

An interactive R Shiny application to monitor and visualize **water quality parameters** in real-time for the Buriganga River, supporting urban health and climate-resilient decision-making in Dhaka.

**Features:**
- Real-time Water Quality Index (WQI) computation
- Interactive geospatial maps and trend visualizations
- Machine learning-based predictions (XGBoost, Random Forest)
- Temporal trend analysis (10-year WQI data)
- Public health risk assessment dashboards

**Technologies:** R · Shiny · ggplot2 · leaflet · XGBoost · tidyverse
